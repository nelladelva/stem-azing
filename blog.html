Editing

Scientific Editing

Manuscript and grant editing, delivered as tracked changes plus a short editorial report — the same format shown below.

★ Starter — Proofreading & Polish from €120 / 1,000 words
  • Grammar, syntax, and word-choice correction
  • Sentence clarity & verb-tense consistency
  • Hedging language tightened; vague claims made precise
  • Formatting, abbreviations, and reference-style checks
★★★ Advanced — Full Publication Package quoted per project
  • Everything in Starter, plus a substantive structural edit
  • Priority revisions flagged by severity (critical / high / medium / low)
  • Journal recommendations ranked by scope, fit, and impact factor
  • Submission-ready cover letter, customized to the target journal

Every edit ships as a tracked-changes document plus a short editorial report like the one the hero example above is drawn from — so you can see exactly what changed and why.
