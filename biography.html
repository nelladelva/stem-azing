Biography · Dr. Nella C. Delva, PhD

A scientist who teaches, writes and reads the data.

I am a neuroscientist with 10+ years of research in cancer biology, neuroscience and stem-cell models, a U.S. Fulbright Fellow, and a first-generation scholar from Haiti. I have done the work I now help others with: running the experiments, analysing the data, publishing it through peer review and mentoring the next scientists.

Data analysis & interpretation

for biotech and life-science companies

  • I know how the data is made. Gene-expression and in vivo work at Moffitt Cancer Center; iPSC-derived neurons and quantitative imaging at the Max Delbrück Center.
  • NeuroPenetrance: a six-gene whole-blood RNA signature, trained on one cohort and tested unchanged on seven others (779 people). AUC 0.61, above chance in 8 of 8. Reported as it is, not as I wish it were.
  • Python (pandas, scikit-learn), SQL and BigQuery, R, GraphPad Prism, ImageJ and QuPath; Plotly, Looker Studio and Power BI dashboards.
  • Data Analytics and Data Science & AI training at Le Wagon Berlin (2025 – 2026).
  • Results explained to scientists and to decision-makers who are not.
