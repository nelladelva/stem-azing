One idea, three audiences

The question at the heart of my research, explained three ways.

Can inflammation in the body affect depression?

For a student

high school · first-year college

When your body fights an infection, it sends out alarm signals. In some people those alarms stay on for a long time, and the brain hears them too. That can make you feel tired, slow and low, a bit like being sick without the fever.

For a curious reader

the public · patients · families

Depression isn't one illness. For about one in five people, it seems linked to an immune system that stays switched on. A routine blood sample may one day help doctors spot those people and choose treatment better.

For a research team

academic · clinical · biotech

A six-gene whole-blood RNA signature, trained on one cohort and tested unchanged on seven others (779 people), separates MDD from controls at AUC 0.61, above chance in 8 of 8, consistent with an inflammatory subgroup.
