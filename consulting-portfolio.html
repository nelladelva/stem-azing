ML Predictive Analysis Report

A full machine learning pipeline predicting sleep disorder risk (high vs. low) from sleep, stress, age, cortisol, BDNF, CRP and IL-6. Validated with 5-fold cross-validation and a held-out test set; feature importance and partial dependence connect the model back to the biology.

ModelAUC-ROCAccuracyCV score
Logistic regression (baseline)0.79172.3%0.784 ± 0.04
Gradient boosting0.83476.7%0.826 ± 0.03
Random forest (selected)0.84778.0%0.839 ± 0.03

Key findings

  • Sleep duration and stress score carry 50% of the model's discriminative power; both are modifiable targets.
  • Below 6 hours of sleep, predicted risk rises sharply; the 6–7 hour window is the highest-leverage zone.
  • Cortisol and BDNF add independent predictive value beyond sleep and stress.
  • Sleep duration + stress + cortisol capture 68% of the full model's predictive power, a lightweight screening option.

Delivered: full report (Word + PDF), Jupyter notebook, 6 figures, model evaluation spreadsheet and CSVs, 2 rounds of revisions.
